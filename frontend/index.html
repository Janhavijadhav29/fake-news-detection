      How it works

      1. Input — paste text, give a URL, speak, or upload a CSV.
      2. Preprocess — lowercase, remove URLs, punctuation, numbers and stopwords.
      3. Vectorize — TF-IDF converts words into numeric features.
      4. Classify — the best of 4 trained models predicts REAL or FAKE.
      5. Explain — key words and warning signs show why.
      6. Store — every result and feedback is saved in SQLite.
